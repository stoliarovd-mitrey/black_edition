Start setup

Before you start

Your glasses arrive assembled and should have some charge. Before you use them, pair them with your phone using the Meta AI app and a Meta account. This guide walks you through the full process for Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer (Gen 2).

What you’ll need

Your phone, Wi-Fi, a USB-C charging cable and a Meta account. No computer or memory card is needed.

How long it takes

Allow about 10 minutes if you already have an account. A software update may take another 15 minutes.

One account at a time

Your glasses can be linked to one Meta account at a time. Reset them before giving them to a new owner.

01Meta AI on your phone

Step 01

Install the app

Scan the QR code inside the box to open the relevant app store. If Meta AI is already installed, the code may take you straight to device setup.

  • Install Meta AI from the App Store.
  • Check the app listing for the iOS version currently required.
  • If the app is unavailable in your country or region, check the official availability information before continuing.
02Your Meta account

Step 02

Sign in

Open Meta AI and sign in to your Meta account, or create an account in the app using your email address, Facebook or Instagram.

  • Use an email address you can access in case you need to recover your account.
  • Enter your name and date of birth when prompted.
  • You must meet the minimum age requirement for your region, usually 13.
  • The app may ask you to choose a Meta AI username. This is visible if you share prompts publicly.
03Glasses and charging case

Step 03

Prepare your glasses

Remove the plastic tab between the glasses and the charging case. There may also be a small protective film beneath the nose bridge. Remove any packaging covering the charging contacts.

  • Take the glasses out of the case.
  • Slide the power switch on the left arm toward the lenses to turn the glasses on.
  • The notification light flashes white during startup, then turns green when the glasses are ready.
04The case’s pairing button

Step 04

Enter pairing
mode

Put the glasses back in the case and gently press them into place so the nose bridge sits on the charging contact. If they are not seated correctly, pairing mode may not start.

  • A flashing blue light on the case means the glasses are ready to pair.
  • If the light does not flash blue, press and hold the button on the back of the case for at least 5 seconds.
  • Keep the glasses in the case while you complete pairing in the app.
05Device setup in Meta AI

Step 05

Pair with
your phone

Turn on Bluetooth and open Meta AI. Tap the glasses or devices icon, then choose the option to add a device. Depending on your app version, you may see “Set up a device” on the home screen. Follow the on-screen instructions.

  • Your glasses can be linked to only one Meta account at a time.
  • The app can manage multiple pairs of glasses. The supported number depends on the app version.
  • Before a new owner pairs the glasses, perform a factory reset.

06Wi-Fi and software updates

Step 06

Connect
and update

Follow the app’s prompts to update your glasses. Keep Wi-Fi enabled: the app uses a temporary Wi-Fi connection to the glasses when importing photos and videos or transferring updates. Meta AI also needs an internet connection through your phone.

  • Open the settings for your glasses and look for the software update option. Menu names vary by app version.
  • To use automatic import, leave the glasses switched on and docked in the case, and enable auto-import in the app.
  • You can still take photos and videos without Wi-Fi and listen to audio over Bluetooth.

02 · Regional availability

What works
where you are

Core functions and online services have different requirements. Check local availability before relying on a particular feature.

Everyday functions

Take photos and videos with the capture button. Use the speakers, microphones and touch controls, or make calls through your paired phone. These functions are generally less dependent on regional AI availability.

App-based features

Import photos and videos, install updates and adjust settings through Meta AI. You’ll need the app, a Meta account and the appropriate phone permissions and connections.

AI and connected services

Meta AI, live translation, music recognition and messaging integrations may depend on your country, language and connected services. Check each feature’s availability in your region.

03 · Troubleshooting

If something
isn’t working

Start with the simplest checks. Try a restart before considering a factory reset, which deletes the photos and videos stored on your glasses.

Glasses not found
Check that the glasses are charged, switched on and seated in the case. Hold the case button for at least 5 seconds until the light flashes blue. Allow up to 10 seconds for the glasses to appear in the app. On iPhone, if an old connection is listed under Bluetooth, tap the information icon and choose Forget This Device, then pair again in Meta AI.
Still not pairing?
Close and reopen Meta AI, then restart your glasses and phone. Check the app’s permissions. If necessary, reinstall the app. If pairing still fails, contact support before resetting the glasses.
Restart the glasses
Slide the power switch off, then back on. Allow a few minutes for the glasses to reconnect. This does not erase your photos or videos.
Force restart
Turn the glasses off. Press and hold the capture button, then switch the glasses on while keeping the button pressed. Release the capture button when the notification light turns red. Your photos and videos are not deleted.
Factory reset
First import any photos or videos you want to keep. A factory reset permanently deletes captures stored on the glasses and removes the account link. With the glasses on and out of the case, close the right arm. Press the capture button 5 times until the notification light turns orange. Press and hold the capture button until the light starts flashing, then release it. Wait for the reset sequence to finish and the light to turn off. Unpair the glasses and set them up again in Meta AI.
Reset the case
Remove the glasses from the case. Hold the button on the back for at least 16 seconds, until the light flashes white. Release the button and wait for the flashing to stop. Put the glasses back in the case and reconnect USB-C power.
Call audio stays on the phone
On iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Call Audio Routing → Bluetooth Headset. On Android, check the glasses’ Bluetooth settings and enable both call audio and media audio.
Unexpected touch inputs
Wet hair, hats or helmets can trigger the touchpad. Keep the touchpad clear and adjust the touch-and-hold shortcut in the app’s gesture settings if needed.
